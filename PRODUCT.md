# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

General public and anyone who encounters this work — students, educators, peers, and potential collaborators. The site is a portfolio piece and a statement: it showcases Jynse Cremers' research and thinking on higher education. Visitors should walk away understanding the argument being made and feeling its urgency.

## Product Purpose

Future Foresight is a course research presentation turned into a living web experience. It makes the case that higher education needs a radical makeover — from credential-delivery to life experience. The site exists to communicate this thesis with force and clarity, using the STEEP framework (Social, Technological, Economic, Environmental, Political) as its analytical backbone.

## Brand Personality

Bold, provocative, urgent. This is not an institution speaking — it is a person with a point of view. The design should feel like a manifesto, not a brochure.

## Anti-references

- Generic university websites: blue/white palettes, stock photography grids, neutral institutional voice
- Student portfolio clichés: minimal white with tiny gray text, centered everything, no energy
- Corporate PowerPoint aesthetic: bullet-point slides, no visual tension, zero personality

## Design Principles

1. **The design makes the argument.** Every visual choice should reinforce the thesis — higher education is overdue for disruption. Nothing should feel safe or settled.
2. **Energy without noise.** Bold does not mean cluttered. The yellow, the big type, the rotated headlines — these earn their place because they carry meaning. Decoration for its own sake undermines the credibility.
3. **Respect the reader.** Dense research content deserves generous spacing and clear hierarchy. The content is serious; the design is bold; neither should undermine the other.
4. **One voice, one author.** This is Jynse's work. The site should feel personal and singular, not like a committee produced it.
5. **Motion serves the argument.** Transitions and reveals should feel like turning a page, not a theme-park ride.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA. Strong contrast throughout (the yellow background and dark text must clear 4.5:1). All interactive elements (the STEEP letter buttons, modal close) keyboard-navigable. Modals trap focus. Reduced motion respected.
