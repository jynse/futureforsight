FUTURE FORESIGHT course - part 01
HI
Higher Education Make over

Offering education to creating life experiences where personal development, agency, real presence and meaning become as vital as hard skills.

WHAT

Starting students crave real presence. But not just in a lecture hall, they want to feel part of one or more communities. They want a good study-life balance and want adaptability and agency over their planning. Young students take care of their mental wellbeing and place this high on the list.

On another level: The public opinion whether a degree is worth the investment, is divided.

Students combine student life with: a job, starting their own business or self development. In our population 3 out of 5 students have a job on the side.

AI is automating our cognitive tasks, so the question of what hard skills to study is there, but answers are lacking.

WHO

Gen Z
Future knowledge workers

Gen Z is the first generation that grew up fully digital and are flooding the job and higher education market.

WHERE

Potential on a global scale.

It is happening, but the actual changes are not there yet. These large institutions can not pivot quickly. Smaller initiatives are seen in specific programs or courses. This trend is not yet part of a vision on education in Belgium.

HOW

Gen Z is only now entering their first jobs or higher education, they react against digital and AI. They look for authenticity.

86% of Gen Z emphasize the need for mentorship and guidance. They value feedback over grading. They expect to see a learning path and have clear expectations set out for them.

They value a more flat hierarchy where a professor is more a coach that helps you grow. They want to see ROI in education.

SOCIAL

Mental Health Crisis

Wellbeing as a business. As a rating for the quality of education. Students demand space for growth inside curriculum. They demand safe social hangout space outside of the curriculum.

Community hunger

Driven by post digital loneliness they form smaller communities with different expectations and needs. They expect the institutions to create spaces therefore but also take initiative to bring them together.

TECHNOLOGICAL

(Hyper) Personalised everything

Adaptive AI tutorships offers the dream of equalised learning, accessible for all at all the time. You can tailor your own learning journey to fit your life. Work in a tempo and skill level that fits you perfectly.

AI hardskills eater

If AI can do the job, what can you bring to the table as a human. What skills will remain? What skills should remain?

ECONOMIC

ROI for everything

Time is money & options are abundant + you can make your future idea. So when Gen Z invests their time (and money) they want to be sure of the return. The trust in the value of a degree has faded. So what does a school actually offer?

Future employment

Employers have already shifted: hard skills can be improved, soft skills are core. The correct attitude, resilience and creativity are highly valued with starters.

ENVIRONMENTAL

Climate anxiety

Students seek meaning and agency in a broken world. They want to feel purpose and agency over their decisions now and in the future.

A holistic mindset

Campus as a living lab where education does not need to take place in an artificial environment. Connect with the environment and each other.

POLITICAL

The trust deficit

People lose trust in public institutions and science. It pushes higher education towards tangible results that connect to the community around it. Where real results feed back to the community.

Academic freedom

More present in the USA and other autocratic countries where politics try to influence the curriculum of higher education. This puts stress on institutions to operate independently.
